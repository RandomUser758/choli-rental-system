Multi-Device Cloud Sync (Real-Time)

Connect with Firebase Firestore (Free) to sync your cholis, bookings, and customers across your phone, tablet, and laptop in real-time. Any changes made on one device instantly reflect on all other devices!

Manual Synchronization Controls

How to get free Firebase in 2 minutes:
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com (sign in with your Google account).
  2. Click "Add project", name it (e.g. choli-rental) and create it.
  3. In the project, click the </> (Web) icon to register a web app.
  4. Copy the firebaseConfig = { ... } object and paste it above!
  5. In the left sidebar, click Firestore Database > Create Database (start in Test Mode).
  6. Done! 100% Free forever within Google's generous limits (50,000 reads/day).
